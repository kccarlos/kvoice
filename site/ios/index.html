KVoice for iPhone: talk instead of typing, in any app

KVoice is a voice keyboard for iPhone. Tap the mic on the keyboard, or press the Action Button, and when you finish speaking the text is typed where you were writing. Speech is recognized on the iPhone by default, with no account and no API key.

Four ways to start

Start a dictation from anywhere on your iPhone

On iOS only a keyboard can type into other apps, and keyboards cannot use the microphone. So KVoice comes in two parts: the KVoice app listens and transcribes, and the KVoice keyboard types the result. Each setup below is done once.

The KVoice keyboardTap the mic above the keys; the text is typed where you left off. The Action ButtonPress to start, press again to stop, usually without leaving your app. Control Center and Lock ScreenOne tap on iPhones without an Action Button, or Back Tap. Siri and ShortcutsSay “Toggle KVoice dictation”, or use KVoice actions in Shortcuts.

Step by step

How to set up KVoice on iPhone

Menu paths below are the ones iOS shows; KVoice's own controls keep their exact names, such as Toggle Dictation.

  1. 1

    Install with TestFlight

    1. On your iPhone, install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store.
    2. Open the KVoice TestFlight invitation, tap Accept, then Install.
    3. Open KVoice. After the welcome page, tap Allow Microphone, then choose how speech becomes text: Apple Speech (built in, fast, no large download) or Whisper Base (on device, 100+ languages).
    4. AI formatting is optional: Apple Intelligence on the device, or your own key later in Settings. Dictation mode never uses AI.

    KVoice listens only while you dictate, and the microphone indicator shows whenever it is on. In the app itself, hold the big mic to talk, or tap to start and tap again to stop; the result is ready to copy or share.

  2. 2

    Add the KVoice keyboard and allow Full Access

    1. Open the Settings app.
    2. Go to General›Keyboard›Keyboards.
    3. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose KVoice.
    4. For dictation, tap KVoice and turn on Allow Full Access, then tap Allow.
    5. In any app, hold the globe key to switch to KVoice.

    Why Full Access? Only dictation needs it: it lets the keyboard talk to the KVoice app on this iPhone. Typing works without it. KVoice never sees what you type with other keyboards, and the KVoice keyboard sends nothing off the device.

    The same steps are in the KVoice app under Settings›Set up the KVoice keyboard.

  3. 3

    Dictate from the keyboard

    1. In any text field, switch to the KVoice keyboard and pick a mode chip, such as Dictation.
    2. Tap the mic above the keys. KVoice opens and starts listening (the keyboard says Opening KVoice…).
    3. Go back to your app: swipe right along the bottom edge, or tap the back link at the top left. Keep talking; the keyboard shows Listening… and a timer.
    4. Tap the red stop button on the keyboard. When it is transcribed, the text is typed once, where you left off.

    For a few minutes afterwards, no app switch. After a dictation, KVoice keeps the microphone ready in the background (5 minutes by default; Settings›Keep listening for the keyboard offers Off, 1, 5, 10 or 15 minutes), so the next tap on the mic records right away. The microphone indicator stays on while it waits; the keyboard's power button (End KVoice session) ends it at any time.

  4. 4

    Set the Action Button to Toggle Dictation

    On iPhones with an Action Button.

    1. Open Settings›Action Button.
    2. Swipe to Controls and choose KVoice › Toggle Dictation.
    3. Press once to start dictating, and again to stop.

    You can also swipe to Shortcut and pick Toggle Dictation under KVoice; it works the same way.

    KVoice tries to start listening in the background; if iOS does not allow it, KVoice may open for a moment. Switch back and keep going.

  5. 5

    Press, speak, press again

    The first press of the Action Button (or the Toggle Dictation control) starts recording, and a Live Activity appears in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen:

    • Recording with a timer and a red Stop button; compact, the Dynamic Island shows a red microphone and the timer.
    • Press again: Transcribing…, then Formatting… if the mode uses AI.
    • Copied with a two-line preview: the text is on the clipboard and in History, and typed for you if the KVoice keyboard is open.

    If KVoice then keeps listening, the Live Activity shows KVoice ready with a countdown and an End button. Dictations started from the keyboard do not show a Live Activity.

  6. 6

    Control Center, the Lock Screen and Back Tap

    No Action Button? Put Toggle Dictation here instead: tap once to start, again to stop.

    • Control Center: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control and choose Toggle Dictation.
    • Lock Screen: touch and hold it, tap Customize›Lock Screen, tap a control at the bottom and choose Toggle Dictation.
    • Back Tap: in Shortcuts, make a shortcut with KVoice › Toggle Dictation, then pick it in Settings›Accessibility›Touch›Back Tap.

    There is also a Dictate with KVoice control, which opens KVoice and starts recording, and a Dictate widget for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, with a mode of your choice.

  7. 7

    Use Siri and Shortcuts

    KVoice's actions work as soon as it is installed. Say to Siri:

    • “Toggle KVoice dictation” : start, and say it again to stop and copy the text
    • “Dictate with KVoice” : open KVoice and start recording
    • “Stop KVoice” : stop the dictation in progress and return its text

    In the Shortcuts app, KVoice offers Toggle Dictation, Dictate with KVoice, Stop KVoice Dictation, Begin Dictation and Transcribe Audio, so you can hand the text to other actions.

    Advanced: if Toggle Dictation does not work

    Make a shortcut that records with the Shortcuts app instead:

    1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to create a shortcut.
    2. Add KVoice › Begin Dictation.
    3. Add If, and set it to: Outcome is Record.
    4. Inside the If, add Record Audio. Set Start to Immediately and Finish to On Tap.
    5. Below it, add KVoice › Transcribe Audio with Audio set to Recorded Audio. Mode is optional.
    6. Below it, add Copy to Clipboard. End If closes the block.
    7. Name the shortcut, then open Settings › Action Button, choose Shortcut and pick it.

Your own

In the Modes tab, tap New Mode: set a name, instructions, language, AI provider and transcription engine.

If an AI mode says Needs AI setup, its provider cannot run: Apple Intelligence is off or not supported, or a cloud provider has no key. Fix it in Settings › AI formatting, or use Dictation. If an AI step fails, KVoice keeps the transcript so nothing you said is lost.

Engines and providers

On the device by default, the cloud only if you choose

Speech to text

Apple Speech (on device; a small model per language the first time); Whisper Tiny, Base, Small or Large v3 Turbo (on device, 100+ languages, language detection, translation to English); or your own key for OpenAI, Groq, Google AI Studio, Google Vertex AI or any OpenAI-compatible transcription endpoint.

AI formatting

Apple Intelligence runs on the device with no key, on supported iPhones. Or bring your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI Studio (Gemini), Google Vertex AI, Groq, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible service.

Asked first

Before anything goes to a cloud provider for the first time, KVoice shows which provider and what will be sent, and asks. Decline and it stays on the device. Keys stay in the iOS Keychain.

History

Every dictation stays on your iPhone

History keeps the audio, the raw transcript and the formatted result. Search transcripts, copy, share, re-run with another mode, or delete.

Set how long it is kept in Settings › History: Forever, 30 days or 7 days. Turn off Keep audio recordings to delete each recording as soon as it is transcribed.

FAQ

KVoice for iPhone: questions

Why does the keyboard open KVoice?

iOS keyboards cannot use the microphone, so the KVoice app records for them. Tap the mic in the keyboard, KVoice opens and listens, swipe back to your app, and the text is typed where you left off. For a few minutes afterwards the keyboard can start the next dictation without switching apps (Settings › Keep listening for the keyboard; choose Off to turn this off).

Why is the microphone indicator on?

KVoice is recording, or it is keeping the microphone ready for the keyboard after a keyboard dictation. Nothing is recorded or saved during that wait until you start a dictation. End it from the Live Activity, or set Keep listening for the keyboard to Off.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. KVoice works on device with Apple Speech or Whisper, and Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it. API keys are only for cloud providers you choose yourself.

Does it work without Full Access?

Typing works, but dictating from the keyboard does not; the keyboard shows Allow Full Access to dictate. The Action Button, Control Center and Siri do not need it, and their result is copied to the clipboard.

Does it run on iPad?

KVoice is currently built for iPhone, running iOS 26 or later.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › History › Delete All History, or delete the app. Remove API keys in Settings first: iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
