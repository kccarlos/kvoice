The first release of KVoice on the Mac App Store. Speak once and get finished text typed into the app you are using: dictation on its own, or with an AI action that cleans up, polishes, turns your words into a message, email, notes or TODO list, answers a question, or translates. On-device transcription with Whisper, Parakeet and other local models, or Apple Speech on macOS 26 or later. AI Actions run on Apple Intelligence on this Mac or on your own endpoint. English and Simplified Chinese interface.
