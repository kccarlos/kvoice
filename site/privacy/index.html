Privacy

KVoice is built so your voice and your words stay on your device unless you choose otherwise. This page summarizes what the Mac and iPhone apps store, what they can send, and where. The full policies in each app's repository are the authoritative versions.

The short version

  • No servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer receives none of your data.
  • Speech is transcribed on your device. Audio is not uploaded, unless you choose a cloud transcription service yourself on iPhone.
  • AI is optional. Only when you turn it on with an online service does that dictation's text (or, for cloud transcription on iPhone, its audio) go straight from your device to that service, never through us.
  • API keys stay local: in a file only your user account can read on the Mac, and in the iOS Keychain on iPhone.
  • Diagnostics stay on the device and record states and timings, never your words.

KVoice for Mac

What stays on your Mac

  • Audio while you dictate is held in memory and discarded once the text is inserted, unless you turn on keeping recordings (off by default).
  • History (on by default, can be turned off) is stored under ~/Library/Application Support/kvoice/, for as long as your retention setting says; Clear All deletes it.
  • Speech models, settings and the Dictionary stay on the Mac. The Dictionary is given to the local speech model as a hint and never leaves it.
  • API keys live in a file readable only by your user account (mode 0600), never in the settings file.

What can leave your Mac

  • Speech model downloads over HTTPS from huggingface.co, at revisions pinned in the app, each file checked against a manifest inside the app. No audio, text, settings or identifiers of yours are sent. Apple Speech's language files are downloaded and managed by macOS.
  • AI Actions (off by default): when on, each transcript and the action's instructions go to the engine you chose, with your user profile and, only for actions you set up that way, clipboard or selected text. With Apple Intelligence on the Mac, nothing leaves it; with an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, only that endpoint receives it; Private Cloud Compute (App Store edition only) is processed on Apple's servers and only when you choose it explicitly.
  • Checks you start: Verify & Save and Test Active Configuration send one short fixed test request, never a transcript, to the engine being checked.

With AI Actions off, KVoice is designed to make no network requests beyond model downloads and the checks you start by hand. Remote endpoints must use HTTPS; plain HTTP is accepted only for localhost.

KVoice for iPhone

What stays on your iPhone

  • Recordings happen only while you dictate. While KVoice keeps the microphone ready for the keyboard, nothing is recorded or saved until you start a dictation. By default a recording is kept in History; turn off Keep audio recordings to delete each one once it is transcribed.
  • Transcripts and formatted text are kept in History, which can be limited to 30 or 7 days; Delete All History removes everything. Deleting KVoice deletes history, recordings, modes and settings.
  • API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain only, and sent only to the provider they belong to. iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted, so remove your keys in Settings first if you want them gone.
  • The KVoice keyboard records no audio, sends nothing over the network, and does not keep or share what you type. Without Full Access it is a plain keyboard; with it, it talks only to the KVoice app on the same iPhone.

When data leaves your iPhone

  • Cloud transcription, if you choose OpenAI, Groq, Google AI Studio, Google Vertex AI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you enter: each dictation's audio goes to that provider.
  • Cloud AI formatting, if you choose OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI Studio, Google Vertex AI, Groq, OpenRouter or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint: the transcript and the mode's instructions go to that provider.
  • Downloads: Whisper models from the WhisperKit model repository on Hugging Face, and Apple Speech language models from Apple. They send none of your dictation.

Before anything goes to a cloud provider for the first time, KVoice shows which provider and what will be sent, and asks; if you decline, it stays on device. Withdraw at any time with Settings › Privacy › Stop Sending to provider.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages: no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts or fonts. As the host, GitHub may log requests for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue in the app's repository: KVoice for Mac or KVoice for iPhone. Please do not post API keys or dictation you want to keep private.
