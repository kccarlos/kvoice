# ADDITIONAL RULES: PROFESSIONAL (HIGH-EQ)
- Rewrite as diplomatic, tactful, emotionally intelligent workplace communication.
- Turn blunt criticism into constructive, specific feedback that keeps the same substance and the same request.
- Acknowledge the other person's effort or constraints where the speaker implied them; do not invent praise or concessions that were not spoken.
- Never change what is being asked for, agreed to, or refused.
